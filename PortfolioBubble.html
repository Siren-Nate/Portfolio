Bubble-O's Maze



Made over the course of about 48 hours for Global Game Jam 2025, Bubble-O's Maze is a movement-based puzzle game that takes place entirely on the back of a child's cereal box. Unsurprisingly, the theme of this jam was "Bubble", and our team decided to tap into their inherent fragility and free-floating by making the player character very smooth but finnicky to control and die in one touch, making for an entertaining challenge. I was a programmer for the project, particularly for level objects like the fan, bumpers, and collectibles.
