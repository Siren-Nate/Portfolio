Get Off My Ice!



Get Off My Ice! is a two-player wrestling game inspired by some physics-based minigames in Mario Party. Players control two penguins on the same iceberg as it rapidly breaks apart, trying to force the other off into the unforgiving waters while avoiding the same fate and collecting fish. I sculpted all of the 3D models from scratch, including the penguins, icebergs, fish, rocks, and elephant seal. I also helped with code and programming where necessary.
