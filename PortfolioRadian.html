Radian Reflex



Radian Reflex is a 2D space shoot-'em-up in which you have the power to temporarily slow down time for all enemies, while simultaneously speeding up your own ship. This game was made to experiment with a minimalist approach to graphics, such as distinct enemies and easily recognizable HUD elements.
